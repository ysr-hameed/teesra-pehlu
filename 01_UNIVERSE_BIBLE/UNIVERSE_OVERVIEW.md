# TEESRA PEHLU — Universe Bible

Genre: Hindi detective thriller, murder mystery and psychological suspense.
Setting: Devgarh, a fictional modern Indian metropolitan city.

Core concept: Every case has another perspective. The obvious explanation may not be the truth.

Rules:
- All stories are fictional.
- Investigations must follow believable logic.
- Show fair clues before revealing the solution.
- Suspects must have credible motives and opportunities.
- The detective can make mistakes and correct them.
- Avoid convenient coincidences and unexplained solutions.

Standard episode duration: approximately 10 minutes.
Publishing target: one episode per week.
Most episodes should be self-contained.
