# Character Identity Lock

Use the approved master reference image as the authoritative identity source.

Preserve the exact:
- Face and facial proportions
- Eyes, nose, lips and jawline
- Skin complexion
- Hair and facial hair
- Apparent age and body proportions
- Signature clothing and accessories

Do not redesign, beautify, age or de-age the character.
Maintain identity across all angles, expressions, poses, lighting and scenes.
